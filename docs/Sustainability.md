# Sustainability & ESG Strategy

## Environmental, Social, and Governance (ESG) Roadmap
- **Energy Conservation**: Phased LED lighting retrofits, smart HVAC thermostat scheduling, and energy audits.
- **Water Management**: Installation of water-saving aerators, rainwater harvesting pilots, and linen reuse programs.
- **Waste Reduction**: Elimination of single-use plastics in guest rooms and F&B outlets, alongside organic food waste composting.
- **Social Impact**: Community engagement programs, sourcing local labor and produce, and fair labor practices.
