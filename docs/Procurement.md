# Procurement Department Assessment

## Supply Chain & Vendor Management
- **Vendor Evaluation**: Quarterly performance reviews of suppliers based on pricing, quality, delivery timeliness, and reliability.
- **Inventory Control**: Just-in-time (JIT) inventory management for high-turnover items to reduce storage holding costs.
- **Sustainable Sourcing**: Prioritizing local suppliers and eco-friendly vendors to support regional economy and ESG goals.
- **Contract Negotiation**: Leveraging bulk purchasing agreements across regional hotel networks for cost savings.
