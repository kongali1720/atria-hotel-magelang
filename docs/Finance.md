# Finance Department Assessment

## Financial Performance Optimization
- **Revenue Management**: Dynamic pricing strategy based on demand forecasting, competitor set analysis, and seasonal trends.
- **Cost Control**: Rigorous monitoring of departmental operating expenses (OPEX) and variance analysis against monthly budgets.
- **CAPEX Governance**: Strict approval workflows and ROI evaluation for capital expenditure projects (e.g., equipment upgrades, tech modernization).
- **Audit & Compliance**: Transparent financial reporting and regular internal audits.
