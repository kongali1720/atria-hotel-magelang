# Information Technology (IT) Assessment

## Digital Infrastructure & Systems
- **Property Management System (PMS)**: Upgrading to cloud-native PMS architecture for seamless multi-departmental data synchronization.
- **Network & Wi-Fi**: High-speed bandwidth expansion across guest rooms, meeting spaces, and public areas.
- **Guest Tech Touchpoints**: Integration of smart room controls (IoT), digital concierge tablets, and secure payment gateways.
- **Data Backup & Recovery**: Automated daily cloud backups and disaster recovery protocols.
