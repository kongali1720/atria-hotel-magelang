# Food & Beverage (F&B) Assessment

## Outlet Optimization & Quality Control
- **Menu Engineering**: Regular analysis of contribution margin and popularity of dishes across restaurant, lounge, and banqueting services.
- **Food Waste Management**: Tracking kitchen food waste and implementing inventory forecasting to minimize over-purchasing.
- **Banquet & MICE Efficiency**: Streamlining event setups and service delivery timelines for corporate meetings and wedding banquets.
- **Hygiene & Safety**: Strict adherence to HACCP standards and regular sanitation audits for all food preparation areas.
