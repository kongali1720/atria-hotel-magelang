# Human Resources (HR) Assessment

## Talent Management & Development
- **Recruitment & Retention**: Addressing skilled labor shortages through local hospitality school partnerships and competitive compensation packages.
- **Training Programs**: Mandatory monthly customer service, conflict resolution, and digital tool training for all staff.
- **Performance Appraisals**: Quarterly key-result-area (KRA) evaluations tied to incentive bonuses.
- **Employee Wellness**: Promoting a healthy work-life balance and mental health support initiatives.
