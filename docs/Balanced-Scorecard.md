# Balanced Scorecard Framework

## 1. Financial Perspective
- **Objective**: Maximize profitability and asset yield.
- **Metrics**: RevPAR growth, GOP margin (>35%), and ROI on CAPEX investments.

## 2. Customer Perspective
- **Objective**: Deliver exceptional hospitality and personalized guest experiences.
- **Metrics**: Guest Satisfaction Score (GSS > 90%), repeat guest retention rate, and net promoter score (NPS).

## 3. Internal Business Processes
- **Objective**: Streamline back-of-house and front-of-house workflows.
- **Metrics**: Housekeeping room turnaround time, preventive maintenance completion rate, and F&B order accuracy.

## 4. Learning & Growth
- **Objective**: Empower staff through continuous training and digital adoption.
- **Metrics**: Training hours per employee, staff turnover rate (<15%), and internal promotion ratio.
