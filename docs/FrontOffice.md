# Front Office Department Assessment

## Operations & Guest Services
- **Check-in/Check-out Velocity**: Target average waiting time under 3 minutes per guest during peak hours.
- **Digital Integration**: Rollout of mobile check-in capabilities and digital key integration to reduce front desk congestion.
- **Upselling Protocols**: Structured front desk upselling program for room upgrades to drive ancillary revenue.
- **Guest Feedback Loop**: Immediate logging and resolution protocol for guest complaints within 15 minutes.
