# 📖 Overview

Welcome to the **ATRIA HOTEL MAGELANG – Strategic Operational Excellence & Business Transformation Master Plan (2026–2028)** repository.

This repository presents a comprehensive hotel operational assessment framework designed to evaluate current performance, identify improvement opportunities, and provide a structured roadmap toward operational excellence.

The report follows internationally recognized business analysis and management frameworks, including SWOT, PESTEL, KPI, Balanced Scorecard, Risk Management, Preventive Maintenance, Sustainability, and Digital Transformation.

---

# 🎯 Vision

To establish a practical, measurable, and sustainable operational excellence framework that supports long-term business growth, guest satisfaction, and organizational resilience.
